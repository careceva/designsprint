The two moves, as review tiles

The Overview's "Two moves worth…" card, redrawn so each tile carries a status and an action, and holds the review content: the company's spend, a document, the current supplier, and the way to Peter. Eight versions, each correcting the one before. Version 8 is what is built into the exploration: after version 5 the direction changed — the typing moves to the spend page, and the row carries spent, saved and the status.

Version 1

Everything, in every tile

The screenshot's form, pasted into both tiles at once: benchmark sentence, spend field, drop box, supplier, Not relevant, Save, Send for review.

What's wrong

  • Two ways to give one number (type it, drop it), both equally loud.
  • Save and Send look the same weight; the user has to work out the difference.
  • Seven lines of explanation for two fields.

Version 3

One field

Cut to the bone: a visible "Add your spend" on the collapsed row, one field when open, the field itself is the drop target, one button. Typing saves on its own.

What's wrong

  • Peter gets a number with no supplier to compare it against.
  • Dropping a file on a text field is something nobody tries.
  • Two actions live on the row (open, and the CTA) and they do the same thing.

Row and sheet

Version 5

One open, one primary

An accordion, so one form is ever visible. Collapsed: mark, name, the benchmark spend, the rate, the status. Open: a spend field with "Add an invoice instead" beside its label (the whole tile also takes a dropped file), the supplier, and an action row where Send to Peter is the one primary. Save keeps a figure without involving anyone; Not relevant swaps the move for the next area, with Undo at the card's foot.

Built, then superseded by 6
What changed from 1–4
  • One number, one field. The document is a way to fill it, not a second input.
  • Status is a fact, not a mode: Not reviewed, Saved, With Peter. The promise ("replies within two working days") appears where the Send button was, once it is true.
  • After Save the tile prints her figure and the saving at her spend, in the app's estimate ink, and the whole page follows (the tune model).
  • Three lines of help in total.

Version 6

Version 5's row, no form

The direction after version 5: the row stays, the typing moves to the spend page. Name with the spend under it, the saving and the status on the right, a chevron as the only mark — the whole row is the way in.

Built, then superseded by 7
What changed from 5
  • Nothing opens in place; the row goes to the spend page. No Save, no Send, no "With Peter".
  • Status is Not reviewed or Reviewed. The spend line prints her figure in the slider's blue once she has set it.
  • The title is the sum of the two savings; the arc went — its figure divided every area's saving by two areas' spend.
