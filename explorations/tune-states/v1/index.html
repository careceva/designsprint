Adjusted-number state

Four treatments of the same moment: a slider has been dragged and the Saved and Spend figures are now the user's projection, not invoice facts. Each page is hardcoded and self-contained.

ACurrentLabel flips to "a year", Saved gains a plus, a pill appears in the app bar. Spend has no cue. BGhost baselineThe adjusted figure turns brand blue; the invoice figure stays beneath it, small and struck through. CDeltaThe figure keeps its place; a blue chip carries the change itself. The touched card tints. DScale echoThe card repeats the slider's own black-and-blue track under the figure, with the same dot the slider row shows.

How to test. Open a version, drag a slider under "Savings still open, by category", then look away and back at the three cards. The question is whether you can tell, without reading, which figures you changed. The pill in the top bar resets everything.
