What the timeline changes

The right sidebar is the same component everywhere but earns its place differently per concept: shopping history in the Storefront, a transaction log in the Statement, category-scoped in the Spine, a decision trail in Today, and an audit log citing report sections in the Report. Acting in the main view prepends a new entry — that loop is the point.

From the market

Vinst (Visma NO) and Seiva (SE) both sell breadth, a named human, and proof: 50–100+ agreements, purchasing coaches, bonus, benchmarking, free trial. Each variant surfaces a different subset so you can see which framing fits Advantage.
