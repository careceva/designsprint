# Advantage 3D category icons – two-colour

Eight icons, one per procurement area, with Ultramarine accents. SVG for design and the web, PNG at 1024 px, transparent background.

Rules: one angle, light from the left, everything faces right, a soft footprint shadow, no tile behind the icon.

`svg-small/` holds a small cut of each icon for 18–44px. It has no footprint shadow, no white edges and no details under two pixels (the toolbox label, the truck hubs, the binder rings, the bin's lid seam), and it is cropped to the object. The binder spines and the bin handle are kept and drawn heavier, because they are what make those two readable. Use the full SVG from 48px up.
