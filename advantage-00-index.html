Wired vs stubbed

Applies to both current builds. Know this before demoing.

Actually works

  • All navigation, routing, browser back, deep links, and middle-click on nav items
  • Persona switch — every figure, state and page re-renders
  • Activate / request offer / approve, with confirmation, success state and undo window
  • Favourite an agreement; it appears in the saved destination
  • Editing a notification rule: threshold, channel, cadence, quiet hours
  • Snooze, mark read, mark all read, severity and unread filters
  • Sending a message — optimistic append, thread state, unread counts
  • Starting a thread from an agreement, carrying its context
  • Activity feed gains a real entry when you act, with filters and counts
  • Card/row view switching, category filters, sort (Ledger)
  • Keyboard: arrows, ↵, ⌘K, Esc, ⌘↵ to send (Ledger)
  • Tile reorder and reset (Bento)

Stubbed — shows a toast or dialog, does nothing

  • Document and price-list downloads
  • File attachment in the composer
  • Forward a thread to a colleague
  • Share / notify my buyers
  • Opening a category guide
  • Punchout to the supplier's ordering system
  • Booking advisor time
  • The data-connection flow (a modal placeholder only)

Not present at all

  • Authentication — the persona switch is the only identity
  • Any backend; nothing persists past a page reload
  • A browsable list of all 112 catalogue agreements (12 are modelled, the rest render generically)

Open gaps, ranked

From the competitor inventory: Vinst and Seiva show no evidence of notifications, messaging, document storage, roles and permissions, ordering workflows, or reporting and export. Four of those are now built here. The rest are open.

Build next

  1. Multi-user, roles and approvals. Every build assumes one person is the company. No way to invite a colleague, delegate an approval, or attribute an action to a person. Largest structural gap.
  2. The data connection flow. Stage 02 is stubbed everywhere, and it is the whole conversion path for a new customer. The pieces exist in advantage-onboarding.html.
  3. Rules that generate work items, everywhere. Built once, in v7 Today. It is the mechanic that makes tomorrow's dashboard non-empty and it is missing from both current builds.
  4. Off-agreement leakage by site or person. "Who is still buying at list price, and where" is the most actionable question the product never asks.
  5. Savings methodology and disputes. Every figure claims to be verified from invoices with no explanation of the calculation and no way to challenge it.
  6. Global search and keyboard navigation in the Bento build. Both exist only in the Ledger.

Then

  1. Renewal calendar with owners and dates — expiries are currently chips, not scheduled work.
  2. Bonus settlement detail — accrual basis, payment dates, per-supplier breakdown.
  3. Post-decision tracking — what happened to the offer I requested.
  4. Scheduled reporting and export — monthly PDF, CSV, a link for the accountant.
  5. Nordic language switching (NO / DK / SE) — currently English only.
  6. Data and security transparency — what is read from the ERP, who can see it.
  7. Catalogue completeness — 12 of 112 agreements are modelled; the rest render generically.

Numbering. The series runs v2 → v3 → v4 → v6 → v7 → v8. There is no v5 in that line because advantage-v5-advisor.html is yours; the series skipped the number rather than overwrite it. Files with _ prefixes and parenthetical labels are ones you renamed.

Verification. Every build from v4 onward was render-checked with zero JS errors and no horizontal overflow at 390, 768 and 1440px, across the overview, an agreement page, a category page, notifications, inbox, and the second persona.

Two exceptions. advantage-v4-2-ledger.html and advantage-v6-3-spine.html were recovered from working files after their builds were cut off mid-run, so neither got a final review pass. Both render clean but are the two to test hardest.
