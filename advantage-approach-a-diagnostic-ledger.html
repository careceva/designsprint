03 · Category ledger

Ranked by gap, not by size

Rank is the distance above the negotiated group rate — not the size of the spend. Office supplies is the smallest line on this page at 205.000 kr. and still ranks first, because you pay 19% over the group rate there, while building materials at 2.132.300 kr. is only 9% over. A gap is the part you can actually recover; size only tells you how loud it is. Open a row to see the benchmark logic, the suppliers behind it and the matched agreements — the totals above stay on screen.
