Three things, then close the tab

A stated position, not an omission. This surface has three elements and will never have more. Every additional element here is a bet that the customer will come looking, and that bet loses: a 12-person contracting firm opens this maybe twice a year. Anything worth knowing is sent — to mail, to the chat channel, or into the invoice screen at the moment it matters. If something important is only discoverable here, we have failed to send it.

The primary artefact

The digest

This is the product. It arrives on the first working day of the month, and on the rare day something cannot wait. One decision above the fold, one figure, one action — everything else is below it, where it belongs. Shown as it lands in a mail client, because that is the only place it will ever be read.

Advantage

What this replaces. v2's dashboard, benchmark tray, savings gauge and category drill-downs are all gone. Between them they answered questions nobody was asking on a Tuesday. Six subject lines a year, each with a decision attached, is the same information delivered to somebody who is not looking for it.

Interrupt policy

What is worth interrupting you for

Set the number in kroner. Findings above it reach you the day we see them; findings below it wait for the monthly digest — they are never thrown away. A 4% rise on your largest supplier clears almost any threshold. A new agreement in a category you barely buy clears none of them, and should not.

At 0 kr. everything interrupts, which is how alerting products get muted. Move it up until the number on the right is one you would actually welcome.

A hard cap. If more findings clear the threshold in a month than the cap allows, the largest goes out and the rest fall into the digest. The cap is the promise; the threshold is only a filter.

Above the threshold — a mail the same day, plus a card in the Teams channel. Two channels, one event, no digest wait.
Below the threshold — held for the monthly digest, listed under the fold.
Worth 0 kr. — sync reports, renewed terms with no price change, agreements added in categories you already cover. These never interrupt at any setting, and are not in the digest either. They are in the annual statement, once.
Exempt from the policy — the warning inside your invoice screen. It is not an interruption: you were already placing the order, and it costs one glance.

Point of purchase & chat

Where it reaches you

The most valuable second in the whole product is not a visit to this app. It is the second before an order goes to the wrong supplier. So the intervention lives in the invoice screen, and the small number of events that cannot wait land in the channel that is already open.

1 · Inside the invoice, before it is posted

2 · In the channel, for the two or three a year

Why not a dashboard notification. A bell badge inside an app the customer does not open is a notification that does not exist. Both surfaces here are places the customer is already looking, for their own reasons, at the moment the information is worth something.
