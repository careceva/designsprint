Journey analysis · Design Sprint

Twelve steps, three lanes — and where the prototype stops

The new journey mapped against what the Autopilot prototype already does. Green headers are the user's step, yellow is what the AI contributes, and the bottom row is honest prototype coverage.

The thing to notice

The AI lane goes blank after "Decide"

Steps 9, 10 and 11 — Follow activation, Monitor spend, Prove value — have no AI note at all. The intelligence story is fully worked out up to the decision and empty after it.

And so does the prototype

The same half is missing in the build

Four of the five weakest steps sit after the decision. Two independent artefacts pointing at the same gap is worth taking seriously — the post-decision half is unowned.

The structural change

This is a loop, not a funnel

Step 12 feeds back into step 5. That is a real shift from the old six-step activation funnel, and it is what turns this from onboarding into a service that keeps earning.

Watch this

Step 4 and steps 10–11 are the same data

Before the decision it's potential; after it, verification. Same numbers, opposite job. Building them as one screen is the obvious trap.

What I'd add to the prototype, in priority order

StepWhat's missingWhy it earns its place
07 · Evaluate A side-by-side: current supplier and terms against the proposed agreement — price, terms, switching effort, risk. Today the prototype jumps straight from “here's an opportunity” to “send the message.” It's the actual decision moment, and it's the screen most likely to expose whether people trust the number.
09 · Follow activation A tracker for a switch in progress: request sent → supplier confirmed → rate live → first invoice verified. The AI lane is blank here. Silence after a decision is where trust leaks away.
10 · Monitor spend Leakage view — spend that went outside the agreement, by person or site. The usage bars show the total but never say who. Directly answers the adoption problem, and it's the one thing only you can see and the customer can't.
11 · Prove value Accumulated and paid customer bonus, plus supplier and Transparency Act documentation. Bonus is real money nobody has designed for. Transparency Act is the only item on this journey with a legal deadline behind it.
12 · Prioritise next A ranked “next category” card driven by what already worked, feeding back to step 5. Without it the journey isn't a loop, it's a one-off — and the whole retention argument rests on the loop.

One question for the room

The AI lane is richly specified for finding and recommending, and empty for delivering and proving. That may be deliberate — or it may be that nobody has yet asked what intelligence looks like after the decision. Worth settling before Wednesday's sketching, because the answer decides whether this is an advisory product or an operational one.
